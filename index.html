What we believe
Movement moves energy.

When the body moves, something shifts. Stuck energy loosens, the mind gets quieter, and you leave with more than you came in with. Every class and every practice here starts from that idea.

What happens here

Movement, body, strength.

No mirrors full of strangers, no clanking machines, no feeling watched. Just a calm, women-only space to train, stretch, breathe and grow stronger at your own pace.

Movement

Every way the body moves

Yoga, flow, dance, mobility and free movement. Practices that get you out of your head and into your body.

Body

Awareness and care

Breath, alignment, rest and healing. Learning to listen to the body as much as you train it.

Strength

Strong from the inside

Functional strength that supports how you live, built steadily and safely.

Who she is

One transformational journey.

Aparna's love for movement took her to yoga many years ago, and now to teaching it. Yoga taught her that learning never stops: the deeper she goes, the more there is to learn and absorb. She still sees herself as a student, and expects to be one all her life.

That spirit shapes the studio. She built The Full Circle Studio for women who want to get strong and fit without the typical gym environment, in a space where strength and softness sit side by side and every woman can find the kind of movement that works for her.

Why this studio

Strong, fit, and nothing like a gym.

The Full Circle Studio is a fitness studio only for women. Build a strong, fit version of yourself in a calm space made for you, without the noise, the machines or the feeling of being watched.

Only for women

A space where women can train, sweat, stretch and rest without holding back. Every class, every session, every person on the floor.

Not the typical gym

No rows of machines, no loud music, no crowd. A quiet, welcoming studio where the focus is on you and how you feel.

Built on one belief

Everything here comes back to the idea that movement moves energy. Classes are designed to leave you feeling lighter and stronger, not just tired.

Every kind of movement

Strength, flow, dance, breath and healing practices all under one roof. Try something new without changing studios.

Strength and softness together

You can train hard and still be gentle with your body. We make room for both.

Come as you are

No experience needed and no body type expected. Beginners and seasoned movers share the same floor.

Founder-led

Aparna, a yoga teacher, is in the studio. You learn from the person who shaped the space and what it stands for.

A shared home for teachers

Yoga teachers, dancers and healers also teach here, which keeps the studio full of fresh ideas and new people.

Come and feel it for yourself.
